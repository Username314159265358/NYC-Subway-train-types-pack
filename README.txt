# NYC Subway Divisions Mod

> **⚠️ WARNING:** This mod is 100% **FREE** and open-source. **If you paid money for this pack, you have basically just got scammed lol**

Adds standalone **NYC Subway A Division (IRT)** and **B Division (IND/BMT)** train types to **Subway Builder**. 

Unlike mods that overwrite vanilla trains, both divisions are added as completely separate, standalone train types—making this pack **100% compatible** with other train mods.

---

## Included Train Types

| Feature | NYC A Division (IRT) | NYC B Division (IND/BMT) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Real-World Prototype** | R142A / R62A (Lines 1–7, 42 St Shuttle) | R211 / R160 (Lines A–Z, Shuttles) |
| **Car Dimensions** | 15.54 m × 2.67 m (51 ft × 8 ft 9 in) | 18.35 m × 3.05 m (60 ft × 10 ft) |
| **Train Length (Cars)** | **3 to 11 cars** | **3 to 10 cars** |
| **Capacity per Car** | 185 passengers | 240 passengers |
| **Min Turn Radius** | 24 m (tighter Manhattan/Bronx curves) | 29 m (standard heavy rail) |
| **Track Compatibility** | A Division tracks only | B Division tracks only |

## Installation

### Via Railyard (Recommended)
1. Open **Railyard**.
2. Search for **NYC Subway Divisions Mod**.
3. Click **Install** and enable it in the game's **Settings -> Mods** menu.

### Manual Installation
1. Download the latest `.zip` release from the [Releases](../../releases) page.
2. Extract the `nyc-subway-divisions-mod` folder into your `%APPDATA%\metro-maker4\mods\` directory.
3. Launch **Subway Builder** and enable the mod in **Settings -> Mods**.
